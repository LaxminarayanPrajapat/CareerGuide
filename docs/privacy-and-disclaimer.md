# Privacy Policy and AI Advice Disclaimer

*This page is the product's own privacy and responsible-AI notice. The in-application version of this
text is generated by the server and appears at the top of every career report.*

---

## What this system is

An academic web application that candidates use to complete timed assessments, build a structured
skill profile, and receive an explainable career recommendation with a development roadmap.

It is **not** a recruitment tool, a hiring decision support system, or a psychological assessment.

---

## Information we collect

### Account information
- Full name, email address, account role.
- Password — stored **only** as a bcrypt hash. It is never stored in plain text, never returned by
  any API, and never written to a log.

### Academic profile (optional)
- Institution, department, year of study, batch, roll number, expected graduation year.
- A short career objective and a list of interests.
- Portfolio or LinkedIn URL.

These fields improve batch-level reporting and help a counsellor support you. They are not required
to use the system.

### Assessment activity
- Which test sets you started, which questions you answered, your selections, your flags, how long
  you took, and the resulting scores.
- Per-skill measurements and the weighted history of how those measurements changed.

### Derived data
- Your skill profile and its revision history.
- Generated career reports, including the scoring version and the exact inputs used.
- Learning roadmap items you have marked in progress or complete.

### Technical events
- Sign-in and sign-out times, failed sign-in attempts, account lockouts.
- Administrative actions taken on your account.
- IP address and user agent on security-relevant events.

## How we use it

| Purpose | Data used |
|---|---|
| Score your assessments | Your responses + the stored answer key |
| Build your skill profile | Scored assessments |
| Produce a career report | Skill profile + career catalogue |
| Show your history and progression | Your assessments and reports |
| Support you | Your profile and records (visible to your counsellor) |
| Institutional reporting | **Aggregates only** |
| Security and audit | Sign-in events, administrative actions |

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling for third parties, or automated hiring
decisions.

## Who can see your data

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **You** | Everything, in your own workspace |
| **Your assigned counsellor** | Your profile, assessment results, skill profile and reports |
| **Administrator** | All records, for support and system administration |
| **Management** | **Aggregate figures only.** Individual candidate records are not available to this role — the API refuses the request. |
| **The public** | Nothing |

Access is enforced on the server for every request. Hiding a button in the interface is a
convenience, not the protection.

## AI and your data

If an AI provider is configured, the following happens — and nothing more:

1. Scores, gaps and career rankings are computed **first**, by a deterministic algorithm.
2. Only those already-computed results are sent to the provider, along with instructions not to invent
   figures and not to use diagnostic language.
3. The provider's response is validated against a strict schema, and any career it names that is not in
   your results is discarded.
4. Its text is stored **separately** from the computed results and never alters them.

**If no provider is configured, or the request fails or times out, no AI request is made or claimed.**
Your report is generated from deterministic templates instead, and the interface labels it clearly as
a deterministic report.

You can tell which happened: every report states the provider used, or that none was used.

## Your working-preference results

The working-preference questionnaire is **not** a psychological assessment.

- It produces **indicative preferences** about the kind of work you tend to enjoy.
- It is **not** a personality type, trait model, diagnosis or clinical finding.
- It does **not** measure ability, and it **never** limits which careers you may pursue.
- A score of 50 means "no preference expressed in either direction".

---

## Career compatibility scores — what they are and are not

**They are:** a comparison between the competencies measured for you and the competencies a career
definition requires, using a documented, versioned algorithm.

**They are not:**

- a probability of getting a job,
- a hiring prediction or a hiring decision,
- a guarantee of success in any profession,
- a psychometric norm, percentile or rank,
- validated against real career outcomes (no such validation is claimed).

A low score means *this role lists competencies you have not yet demonstrated in this system* — which
is information, not a verdict. Many skills are learned.

---

## Retention

Your records are retained while your account is active. Deactivating an account blocks sign-in
immediately and invalidates all sessions; assessment history and reports are retained so academic
records remain intact.

For deletion requests, contact the administrator of your deployment.

---

## Security measures

Password hashing with bcrypt · `HttpOnly` session cookies · CSRF protection on every state-changing
request · server-side role and ownership checks on every endpoint · input validation against a strict
schema · rate limiting on credential and expensive endpoints · security and administrative audit
logging with redacted metadata · secrets held only in server environment variables and never exposed
to the browser.

See [`security.md`](security.md) for the full threat model and its known gaps.
